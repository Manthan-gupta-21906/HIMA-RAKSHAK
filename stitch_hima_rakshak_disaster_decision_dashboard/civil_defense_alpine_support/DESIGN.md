## Brand & Style

The visual identity is forged for high-stakes, civil-defense decision workflows across the rugged, vulnerable topography of Jammu & Kashmir. The emotional core balances unquestioned institutional command with calm operational precision. In high-consequence crisis situations, clutter and ambiguity cost lives; the design language therefore removes decorative excess in favor of crisp visual hierarchy, absolute clarity, and instantaneous scanability.

The aesthetic fuses utilitarian command-center ergonomics with sophisticated editorial precision. Grounded in deep graphite and charred slate foundations, the interface relies predominantly on luminous, high-clarity warm light backgrounds that reduce operator eye fatigue over extended operational watches. High-visibility warning accents—derived from alpine hazard standards—cut through neutral card surfaces to denote urgency, verified agency statuses, and imminent geohazard thresholds.

## Colors

The system uses a calibrated tonal structure designed for swift risk triage:

- **Canvas & Neutrals**: Primary layouts rest on `#f9f8f7` with active cards on `#fcfcfc`. Secondary utility trays and panel dividers employ `#ebe7df` and `#e4e1d1` to establish clean separation without hard artificial lines.
- **Deep Graphite & Obsidian**: `#1c1a15` and `#0e1414` function as the high-contrast structural anchors for primary typography, command headers, and active state pill buttons.
- **Alert & Hazard Accents**:
  - `hazard-yellow` (`#ffd400`): Active warnings, advisory banners, avalanche watch zones.
  - `emergency-red` (`#f4472c`): Immediate evacuation, flash flood alerts, bridge closures.
  - `critical-blue` (`#3772ff`): Meteorological and river gauge telemetry anomalies.
  - `alert-green` (`#11d477`): Nominal status, cleared transit corridors, functional sensors.
- **Institutional Badges**: Explicit agency signatures provide source attribution without visual collision:
  - **IMD** (India Meteorological Department): Tactical Blue (`#2752b8`)
  - **CWC** (Central Water Commission): Hydrological Deep Teal (`#037070`)
  - **DGRE** (Defence Geoinformatics Research Establishment): Terrain Ochre (`#c48022`)

## Typography

Inter serves as the primary typographic workhorse across all display, body, and telemetry readouts, selected for its unmatched neutral clarity and optical legibility in dense data tables and map HUD overlays. 

- **Headlines**: Set with tight optical kerning (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) and direct line heights to anchor mission status modules firmly.
- **Body & Data**: Medium weight (`500`) is leveraged for immediate status metrics, while regular (`400`) ensures high readability during multi-paragraph situation briefings.
- **Labels & Agency Attribution**: All technical indicators, sensor codes, and institutional tags use small uppercase tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to enforce distinct visual separation from situational body copy.

## Layout & Spacing

The dashboard runs on a modular 12-column desktop grid with a maximum content boundary of `1440px`. The layout is structured to maintain persistent situational awareness:

- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12 columns with `2.5rem` (`40px`) gutters and `3.5rem` (`56px`) canvas margins. Sidebars for incoming feeds (IMD, CWC, DGRE) take 3 columns, the central interactive tactical map spans 6 columns, and right-hand telemetry/incident feeds claim 3 columns.
- **Tablet / Split View (843px - 1023px)**: 6 columns with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and margins; panels stack into two primary visual zones.
- **Mobile Handheld (up to 428px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` (`16px`) margins; feeds become vertically scrollable cards under a fixed emergency alert header.

Spacing adheres strictly to a foundational 4px/8px rhythm. Internal component gaps use `space-sm` (`8px`) or `space-md` (`16px`), while major module cards leverage `space-xl` (`40px`) inner padding to maintain breathing room and prevent cognitive fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined primarily through crisp structural layering, micro hairline strokes, and deep, low-opacity ambient shadows rather than heavy blurring:

- **Base Canvas**: Solid warm tone (`#f9f8f7`), resting at base elevation without shadow.
- **Command & Telemetry Cards**: Raised surface (`#fcfcfc`) bounded by a subtle border (`1px solid #d9d3c9`).
- **Active Incident Popovers & Tactical Menus**: Elevated with an ambient blur shadow: `box-shadow: 0px 8px 48px 0px rgba(14, 20, 20, 0.08), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(14, 20, 20, 0.04)`.
- **Dark HUD Overlays & Crisis Rails**: Deep `#1c1a15` containers incorporate an inner luminous rim stroke: `box-shadow: 0px 0px 0px 1px rgba(252, 252, 252, 0.12) inset`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an intentional contrast between architectural card geometry and human-interactive controls:

- **Structural Card Enclosures**: Built with crisp, soft contours (`0.25rem` / `4px`) to present a grounded, military-grade civil protection posture.
- **Interactive Action Pills**: Buttons, agency tags, and threat badges expand to pill contours (`32px` to `64px`) to create clear affordance for rapid interaction under high-stress command conditions.
- **Dividers & Hairlines**: Precision lines measuring exactly `1px` (`#d9d3c9`) preserve visual discipline across dense data matrices.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Operational Action**: Fill `#1c1a15`, text `#ffffff`, border radius `64px` (pill), height `40px`, padding `0 24px`. On hover: background `#0e1414` with subtle scale transition.
- **Secondary Action**: Fill `#e4e1d1`, text `#1c1a15`, border radius `64px`, height `40px`, padding `0 20px`.
- **Emergency Action**: Fill `#f4472c`, text `#ffffff`, border radius `64px`, font weight `600`.

### Official Authority Badges (IMD, CWC, DGRE)
- Pill capsule format (`padding: 3px 10px`, radius `64px`, text `badge-micro`).
- **IMD Badge**: Background `#d7e3ff`, text `#13295b`, left dot `#2752b8`.
- **CWC Badge**: Background `#cdf9f9`, text `#024444`, left dot `#037070`.
- **DGRE Badge**: Background `#fdf1d6`, text `#8c380f`, left dot `#c48022`.

### Threat Chips & Status Badges
- Pill styling (`padding: 4px 12px`, radius `64px`, text `label-sm` uppercase).
- **Hazard Warning**: `#ffd400` fill with `#1c1a15` text.
- **Critical Emergency**: `#f4472c` fill with `#ffffff` text.
- **Corridor Clear**: `#11d477` fill with `#054024` text.

### Telemetry & Hazard Cards
- Built on `#fcfcfc` with `1px solid #d9d3c9` border and `4px` corner radius.
- Internal padding: `space-lg` (`24px`) for compact units; `space-xl` (`40px`) for major sector status cards.
- Integrated header contains metric label (`label-md`), status pill indicator, and agency source badge.

### Input Fields
- Capsule profile (`height: 44px`, `border-radius: 32px`, fill `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #d9d3c9`, padding `0 20px`).
- Focus state: `border-color: #1c1a15`, ring `2px rgba(28, 26, 21, 0.12)`.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Controls
- Multi-tone control borders: `1px solid #0e1414` with a soft bottom drop. Check indicators use high-contrast dark graphite fill with pure white icons.
